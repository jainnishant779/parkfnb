FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Write to us from the Help screen in the app.

How do I book a parking spot?

Search the area you're heading to, pick a spot, choose your date and duration, then pay in the app. You get a booking ID straight away.

How do I get in at the gate?

At gates with number-plate cameras, the barrier opens automatically for the vehicle on your booking. Everywhere else, show your booking in the app.

Can I book by the hour or by the month?

Both. Spots can be booked hourly, daily, weekly or monthly, depending on what the owner offers.

Which payment methods are supported?

UPI, cards and FASTag where the location supports it. Every payment comes with a receipt in the app.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, from the booking details screen. The cancellation policy for each spot is shown before you pay.

I have an empty space. How do I list it?

Download the Parkfnb owner app, complete a quick verification, then add your space with photos and pricing.
