# ParkFNB — Demo video (Hindi)

90 second. Visual AI se, voiceover Hindi mein, aur beech ka app wala hissa
**asli screen recording** — wo AI se mat banao, judges pakad lete hain.

```
0:00-0:18  Problem           AI clips
0:18-0:30  Solution reveal   AI clips
0:30-0:55  Product           SCREEN RECORDING
0:55-1:12  Technology        AI + screen
1:12-1:30  Vision + close    AI clips
```

Har AI clip **5-8 second** ka rakho. Usse lamba maangoge to quality girti hai.

---

## Har prompt ke saath ye line jodo

```
photorealistic, cinematic color grading, 16:9, no text overlays,
no logos, no watermarks, no on-screen writing
```

Text AI se mat likhwao — wo gibberish banata hai. Text baad mein editing
software se daalo.

# Voiceover — kaise record karein

**Tone:** confident, dheere. Excited nahi — **sure** lagna chahiye.
Zyadatar log jaldi bolte hain; jaan bujh ke dheere bolo.

**Pauses jahan rakhni hain:**
- "parking." ke baad — 1 second
- "Har baar." ke baad — 1 second
- "wahi asli product hai." ke baad — 1.5 second

**Record kaise:** phone ke earphones ka mic kaafi hai. Kapde se bhare
kamre mein record karo (almari ke paas), khaali kamre mein echo aata hai.

**Free TTS chahiye to:** ElevenLabs par Hindi voices achhi hain, par apni
awaaz zyada authentic lagti hai — ye aapka product hai.

---

# Music

Instrumental, minimal, koi lyrics nahi. 0:00-0:18 dheema aur tense,
0:18 par uthega, 1:19 par khulega.

YouTube Audio Library ya Pixabay Music se free mil jayega. Search:
"corporate inspiring minimal".

**Voiceover se 12-15 dB neeche rakhna** — warna awaaz dab jaati hai.

# Jo cheezein galat ho jaati hain

**AI se number plate mat maango** — wo fake gibberish banata hai. Camera
focus wala shot lo, plate ka text dikhaye bina.

**"Indian" har prompt mein likho** — warna American parking lot banega jo
aapki story se match nahi karega.

**App AI se mat banwao** — fake UI turant pakda jaata hai, aur aapka asli
app already bana hua hai. Wahi dikhao.

**90 second se lamba mat karo** — judges ka attention utna hi hai.
